Experiment 01: baseline ffNN

Dataset:
- xview_recognition.
- 18,746 training samples.
- 13 object categories.
- Images resized to 224x224.
- Training/validation split: 90%/10%, random_state=1, shuffle=True.

Architecture:
- Flatten(input_shape=(224, 224, 3)).
- ReLU activation.
- Dense(13).
- Softmax activation.

Optimization:
- Adam optimizer.
- Learning rate: 1e-3.
- beta_1=0.9, beta_2=0.999.
- amsgrad=True.
- clipnorm=1.0.
- Batch size: 16.
- Maximum epochs: 20.

Callbacks:
- ModelCheckpoint monitoring val_accuracy.
- ReduceLROnPlateau monitoring val_accuracy, factor=0.1, patience=10.
- EarlyStopping monitoring val_accuracy, patience=40.
- TerminateOnNaN.

Validation results:
- Best validation model: epoch 11.
- Best validation accuracy: 38.560%.
- Mean Accuracy: 38.560%.
- Mean Recall: 30.950%.
- Mean Precision: 31.433%.

Reference validation metrics for comparison with Codabench:
- Mean Accuracy: 38.560%.
- Mean Recall: 30.950%.
- Mean Precision: 31.433%.
