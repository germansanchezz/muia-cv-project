Experiment 07: optimizer comparison using the experiment_05 ffNN

Hypothesis:
- Compare SGD with momentum against the Adam optimizer used in experiment_05.

Kept equal to experiment_05:
- Architecture: Flatten -> Dense(512) -> Dense(256) -> Dense(128) -> Dense(13).
- Original, unnormalized image inputs.
- Learning rate: 1e-4.
- Batch size: 32.
- Maximum epochs: 100.
- Same train/validation split.
- Same ReduceLROnPlateau and EarlyStopping callbacks.

Changed:
- Optimizer changed from Adam to SGD with momentum=0.9 and nesterov=True.
- Checkpoint and prediction paths point to experiment_07.

Challenge compliance:
- Feedforward neural network using dense layers only.
- No CNN, transfer learning, data augmentation, dropout, batch normalization,
  class weights or norm penalties.

Results:
- Best validation model: epoch 91.
- Training completed all 100 epochs.
- Mean Accuracy: 49.920%.
- Mean Recall: 44.838%.
- Mean Precision: 49.124%.

Conclusions:
- SGD with momentum improved the main Mean Accuracy over experiment_05 with
    Adam, increasing it from 47.573% to 49.920%.
- Mean Recall and Mean Precision also improved compared with experiment_05.
- The validation accuracy continued to improve at late epochs, so the larger
    training budget was useful for SGD.
- A substantial train/validation gap remained: training accuracy reached about
    78%, while the best validation accuracy was 49.920%.
- The model did not reach the 55% target, but the optimizer change was
    promising enough to justify a focused learning-rate experiment.

Proposal for experiment_08:
- Keep the experiment_07 architecture, unnormalized inputs, batch size,
    callbacks and SGD momentum configuration.
- Change only the SGD learning rate from 1e-4 to 3e-4.
- The goal is to test whether the slow late improvement in experiment_07 is
    caused by a conservative learning rate.
