Experiment 04: baseline ffNN with normalized inputs

Hypothesis:
- Test whether normalizing image values to [0, 1] improves the original
  baseline architecture without adding hidden layers or changing the optimizer.

Architecture:
- Flatten(input_shape=(224, 224, 3)).
- ReLU activation.
- Dense(13).
- Softmax activation.

Changed from experiment_01:
- Training, validation and test images are normalized to [0, 1].

Kept fixed for a fair comparison:
- Adam optimizer, learning rate 1e-3.
- beta_1=0.9, beta_2=0.999, amsgrad=True, clipnorm=1.0.
- Batch size: 16.
- Maximum epochs: 20.
- Same train/validation split.
- Original callbacks monitoring val_accuracy.

Results:
- Training completed all 20 epochs.
- Best validation model: epoch 20.
- Best validation accuracy: 41.280%.
- Mean Accuracy: 41.280%.
- Mean Recall: 34.049%.
- Mean Precision: 36.760%.

Class-level observations:
- Building recall decreased to 55.923%, compared with 82.920% in
  experiment_03, indicating less concentration on that class.
- Small car remained strong, with 78.419% recall.
- All 13 classes obtained non-zero recall, including Fishing vessel,
  Helipad and Pylon.
- The largest remaining weaknesses were Truck (8.714% recall), Helipad
  (11.111%), Storage tank (15.789%) and Motorboat (19.266%).

Conclusions:
- Normalizing the input values while keeping the original baseline architecture
  improved all three comparison metrics relative to experiment_01:
  accuracy increased from 38.560% to 41.280%, recall from 30.950% to 34.049%
  and precision from 31.433% to 36.760%.
- It also clearly improved over experiment_02 and experiment_03 in Mean
  Accuracy, Mean Recall and Mean Precision.
- This is currently the best configuration and should be treated as the
  reference model for the next experiment.
