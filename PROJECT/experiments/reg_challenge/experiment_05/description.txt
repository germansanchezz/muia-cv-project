Experiment 05: Supreme Combination (L2 + BN + Dropout)

Hypothesis:
- Test whether combining a light L2 penalty (1e-4), Batch Normalization, and a 
  moderate Dropout (0.3) creates the most robust model, leveraging fast convergence 
  (BN) alongside maximal overfitting prevention (L2 + Dropout).

Kept equal to baseline:
- Original, unnormalized image inputs.
- SGD with learning rate 3e-4, momentum=0.9 and nesterov=True.
- Batch size: 16.
- Maximum epochs: 100.
- Same train/validation split and callbacks.

Changed:
- Architecture changed to a combined regularization block:
  Flatten -> Dense(512, L2 1e-4) -> BN -> ReLU -> Dropout(0.3) -> 
  [Same block for 256, 128, 64] -> Dense(13).
- Checkpoint and prediction paths point to experiment_05.

Challenge compliance:
- Feedforward neural network using dense layers only.
- No CNN, transfer learning, or classical data augmentation.
- USES a combination of norm penalties, batch normalization, and dropout.

Results:
- Best validation model: epoch 64.
- Training stopped at epoch 89 due to the early-stopping criterion.
- Mean Accuracy: 66.730%.
- Mean Recall: 63.550%.
- Mean Precision: 68.120%.

Conclusions:
- The combination of techniques yielded the best overall performance by far, reaching 
  nearly 67% validation accuracy.
- Batch Normalization successfully stabilized the training and kept gradients healthy, 
  while the combination of light L2 and Dropout effectively kept the network from memorizing 
  the training data.
- This experiment demonstrates that, when appropriately tuned, these regularizers are highly 
  complementary and essential for maximizing the capacity of purely dense networks on 
  complex image data.
